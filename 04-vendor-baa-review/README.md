# Vendor and BAA Review

This module gives small practices a lighter-weight vendor review than a full enterprise TPRM process.

Focus areas:

- Does the vendor touch ePHI?
- Is a BAA signed?
- What SOC 2 or HITRUST evidence status is recorded: provided, not provided, absent, or not applicable?
- Are breach/incident notification terms known?
- Does the vendor use customer data for AI training?
- Are subcontractors known?
- What should be reviewed annually?
